\lett{M}{iserable} offenders, repentant and joyful, faithful and longing for the fulness of all things. We have longed with groaning and anticipation for this Second Edition of the \textit{Prayer Book Hymnal,} a more robust reception of the hymnary tradition into Orthodox use.

Due to our other publications, such as our missal, the inclusion of the full Mass propers for each Festival is no longer. In their place, an expanded selection of hymns as well as liturgical notation have been supplied. We trust this will more aptly provide what is necessary for the choirs in many of our parishes. Additionally, this will prove to be a robust companion to the 2025 Proposed Book of Common Prayer, providing what is necessary for the Offices throughout the Church Year.

The \textit{Prayer Book Hymnal,} especially this Second Edition, represents a major accomplishment not only for Apologia Anglicana but also for me personally. This work, beyond any other, has required a great deal of time and learning. I want to thank my best friend, Lukas, who introduced me to four-part harmony and has trained me in music.

This is our first major publication since my wedding. Through the love, support, and encouragement of \textit{uxoris me{\ae} pi{\ae}, fidelis, ob{\oe}dientis,} I have been able to finish this Second Edition. I see God's providence in our marriage in many ways. However, as relevant to this book, I am thankful for her extensive learning, experience, and training in music. Furthermore, through her, my commitment to the fulness of the Anglican tradition has gained an additional dimension: a vision of chanting the psalms and singing the hymns and carols with my wife and expected child. While my additional obligations have slowed down my work, my family has given me renewed vigour. While all of our works at Apologia Anglicana are dedicated to our faithful shepherds (presently \textsc{John X,} \textsc{Saba,} and \textsc{John}), in a unique way this publication is dedicated especially to my wife, A. J. P.\\
