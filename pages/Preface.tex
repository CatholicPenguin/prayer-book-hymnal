\lett{M}{iserable} offenders, repentant and joyful, faithful and longing for the fulness of all things. We have longed with groaning and anticipation for this Second Edition of the \textit{Prayer Book Hymnal,} a more robust reception of the hymnary tradition into Orthodox use.

Due to our other publications, such as our missal, the inclusion of the full Mass propers for each Festival is no longer. In their place, an expanded selection of hymns as well as liturgical notation has taken their place. We trust this will more aptly provide what is necessary for the choirs in many of our parishes. Additionally, this will prove to be a robust companion to the 2025 Proposed Book of Common Prayer, providing what is necessary for the Offices throughout the Church Year.

This is our first major publication since my marriage to my wife \textit{devota, fidelis, et ob{\oe}diens,} Photini. While my commitment to the fulness of the Anglican tradition has remained unwavering, it has gained an additional dimension: a vision of chanting the psalms and singing the hymns and carols with my wife and expected child. While my additional obligations have slowed down my work, my family has given me renewed vigour, and the love of my wife has made this possible. While all of our works at Apologia Anglicana are dedicated to our faithful shepherds (presently \textsc{John X,} \textsc{Saba,} and \textsc{John}), in a unique way this publication is dedicated especially to my wife.\\
